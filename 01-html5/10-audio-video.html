Audio and video

HTML5 can play sound and video without an extra plugin. Text between the start and end tags is the fallback, shown only when the browser does not understand the element.

These two players use the sample files from the W3Schools HTML media chapter. They need a network connection. To use your own file, put it next to the page and set src to that relative path.

Video

Common attributes: controls, width, height, autoplay, loop, muted. Autoplay is often blocked unless the video is muted, so this example waits for the controls.

<video width="320" height="240" controls>
  <source src="clip.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Audio

Common attributes: controls, autoplay, loop, muted. The file is usually .mp3 or .wav.

<audio controls>
  <source src="clip.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support the audio tag.
</audio>
